Proposed components: onboarding, sandbox and Settings

Eight new components the onboarding spec (Ahmad, 4 Nov 2026) needs and the design system doesn't have yet. Each one reuses existing tokens and parts (Modal, Button, Badge, Field, Tabs) wherever it can. None is used in the dashboard until you approve it. Copy is draft.

1. SandboxBanner and TestTag Proposed

Tells everyone they're in the sandbox, on every page, with one way back. The tag marks every test amount and AWB.

Used in
Top of every page in sandbox; next to amounts, AWBs and the store name
Reuses
dark band colour, App button, badge warn colours

Settings home: cards in groups, each with a live status line and a badge for anything waiting on FLIK. A card opens its page, with one Tabs row per section.

Used in
Settings home (10 cards in 3 groups), then each card's page
Reuses
OptionCard's shadow and hover, Badge, Tabs, SearchField

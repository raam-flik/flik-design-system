# PaymentCards

Two cards on a payment step: the FLIK balance in `app-ink` (navy) and the amount due in `primary-soft`, followed by a details breakdown and Confirm and pay.

**Use** on the last step of a flow that is paid from the balance (Create shipment). If the balance is too low, keep Confirm and pay disabled and offer Top up balance.

**Don't** show the balance anywhere else in the flow.

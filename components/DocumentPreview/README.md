# DocumentPreview

The live preview half of an editor: an `app-hover` panel with Preview tabs and a white paper that updates as the form changes.

**Use** in Create invoice with three tabs: PDF (the invoice document), WhatsApp (the message the customer gets, with the pay link) and Payment page (amount, payment-method logos, Pay button). The PDF shows the merchant, invoice number, dates, Bill to, the headline amount due, the lines and totals.

**Don't** make the preview editable; every change happens in the form.

# DocumentPreview

The live preview half of an editor: an `app-hover` panel with Preview tabs and a white paper that updates as the form changes.

**Use** in Create invoice with three tabs: PDF (the invoice document), WhatsApp (the message the customer gets, with the pay link) and Payment page (the FLIK invoice page the shopper opens). The PDF shows the merchant, invoice number, dates, Bill to, the headline amount due, the lines and totals.

**Payment page** (decided 3 Nov 2026). Mirror the FLIK invoice page's layout, but build it only from design-system parts and colours, never the live page's own styling: a summary card (amount, due date, an Invoice PDF `Button`, To / From / Invoice, a View invoice details `TextLink`); a payment card with a full-width primary `Button` "Pay with FLIK" (the orange F mark, no tile), an "Or" divider, an Email `FormField`, the methods the merchant switched on as one shadow panel of hairline rows (`RadioList`, chosen 3 Nov 2026 over bordered checkout cards): QRIS is one choice; E-Wallets, Virtual accounts and Credit or debit card show their logos and a chevron and open inline; those logos sit in 3 equal 34×22 tiles, 4px apart (no "+N": with more than 3, the last tile slowly crossfades through the rest every 4 seconds; round marks like OVO and DANA at 16px so they fit) with an `app-line-2` border and 4px corners, like card-brand badges (approved 4 Nov 2026, an exception to PartnerLogo's no-frame rule for this strip only). Wallets have Connect (asks for the phone number; a connected one shows the number, its balance and Change); cards list saved cards ("•••• 4821", remove icon when not chosen), BCA debit and "Visa, Mastercard and other" / "Add new card", which opens Add card. The chosen or opened row sits on `app-hover`. Layout stays the Checkout one (centred cards on grey), chosen 3 Nov 2026 over two dashboard-style layouts. Pay now with QRIS opens the QRIS step in the same look: Transaction ID / Pay before / Total, the QRIS code with the F mark and Download QRIS, Check payment status and Choose another method, then How to pay with one accordion row per app and a secondary Pay now `Button`; a `Checkbox` card for Save my information; then the FLIK wordmark in its own colours, 1-Click Checkout and the Terms of use and Privacy policy links. The invoice previews (PDF, email, payment page) use the dark blue `ink` as their primary colour instead of violet: primary buttons, the selected radio, checked checkboxes and links (decided 3 Nov 2026, invoices only; the editor around them stays violet). Don't recolour the wordmark.

**Don't** make the preview editable; every change happens in the form.

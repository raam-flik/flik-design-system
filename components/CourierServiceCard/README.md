# CourierServiceCard

One courier's services in the Choose a courier step: the courier logo and name on the left, a card per service with delivery time, the price for all shipments, the struck-through normal price, and an optional Recommended or Cheapest tag.

**Use** grouped under Regular, Express and Cargo headings. One service is chosen across the whole list (radio). Use real courier logo files in `fl-logo`; until a logo is cleared for use, show its initials on the courier's colour.

**Don't** show a struck price that is not the courier's published rate, or more than one tag per card.

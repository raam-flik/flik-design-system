# FormField

A labelled text field in two densities: 36px with a soft ring for the dashboard, 42px with a 1px border for marketing forms.

**Use** `fl-field` in dashboard dialogs and drawers, `fl-field fl-field--marketing` in marketing forms (contact sales, the funding estimate). Labels sit above the field; hints in `app-muted` below.

**Don't** add helper subtitles under form titles, or show error text in the footer (disable the primary button until the form is valid).

# ResourceCard

A bordered link card for resources, related products and offers; on hover it lifts 2px to a soft shadow and the arrow extends.

**Use** in groups of three ("More from FLIK", offers). `fl-card--alt` fills it with `surface-alt` for cards that sit inside a white band.

**Markup.** The whole card is the link: `<a class="fl-card"><span class="tag">…</span><b>Title</b><p>…</p><span class="more">Action …</span></a>`.

**Don't** nest buttons inside the card, or mix cards of different heights in one row (keep the copy to one title line and two text lines).
